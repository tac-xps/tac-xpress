# Product capability roadmap

The durable advantage of TAC-XPRESS is reliable, understandable operations for the New Delhi–Northeast India logistics corridor. Product work should make service status, exceptions, proof, and customer communication more trustworthy—not add generic dashboard complexity.

## Capability themes

1. **Operational clarity:** dispatch, manifest, fleet, and warehouse surfaces present the next actionable exception.
2. **Customer confidence:** public tracking, preparation guidance and support updates are accurate, scoped, and easy to understand.
3. **Reliable communication:** WhatsApp, email, and ticket workflows are auditable, idempotent, and recoverable.
4. **Data quality:** staff workflows validate inputs, preserve event history, and surface missing or contradictory data early.
5. **Design consistency:** Nordic Lagom tokens and shared components keep every new feature calm and familiar.

Prioritize measurable delivery reliability and support resolution improvements over speculative platform features.
